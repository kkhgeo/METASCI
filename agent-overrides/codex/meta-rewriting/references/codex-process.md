## SW-PROC-004 — revise-global-before-local

- **Rule:** Resolve global problems in logic, flow, and focus before editing local problems in grammar, formatting, and wording.
- **Rationale:** Polishing text that will later move or disappear wastes effort, and local fluency can conceal structural defects.
- **Diagnostic check:** Is grammar or wording being polished before the structure and argument have been stabilized?
- **Action:** Fix the question chain, paragraph functions, sequence, and evidence placement before moving to sentence-level editing.
