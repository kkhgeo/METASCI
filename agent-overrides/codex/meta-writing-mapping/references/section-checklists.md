# Section architecture checklists

These are functional checks, not fixed templates. Paragraph counts, move order,
word limits, headings, and citation practices must follow the current target venue
and study/reporting requirements.

## Title

- Represents the supported scope without implying a broader population, design, or
  causal claim.
- Contains the terms readers need to identify the topic and study.
- Matches the manuscript's actual contribution.

## Abstract

- Makes the problem and rationale intelligible to a reader who has not opened the
  paper.
- States the study purpose or question.
- Gives only the methodological detail needed to interpret the result.
- Reports the principal result with adequate precision.
- States a conclusion warranted by that result.
- Contains no claim, evidence, citation, or cross-reference absent from or dependent
  on unexplained material in the body.
- Follows the venue's current structured/unstructured format and word limit.

## Introduction

- Establishes the relevant problem and context.
- Represents prior knowledge accurately and proportionately.
- Identifies a concrete gap, tension, limitation, or unresolved question.
- Explains why resolving it matters.
- Ends with an explicit purpose, research question, or hypothesis.
- Does not become a comprehensive literature review unless the genre requires it.

## Methods

- Identifies design, setting, sample or materials, procedures, measures, and
  analysis needed for reproducibility and interpretation.
- Explains consequential nonstandard choices.
- Distinguishes prespecified from exploratory analysis when relevant.
- Includes ethics, registration, software, and reporting details when required.
- Has a corresponding path to every reported result.

## Results

- Follows the research questions, hypotheses, or analysis plan in an intelligible
  order.
- Reports findings before interpretation.
- Uses text, tables, and figures for complementary roles rather than duplication.
- Includes uncertainty, denominators, effect sizes, or other field-appropriate
  quantities where available.
- Does not compare results with prior literature; route that move to Discussion.
- Contains no result without a corresponding method.

## Discussion

- Opens by answering the research question from the reported results.
- Interprets rather than repeats results.
- Relates findings to prior literature without overstating agreement or conflict.
- Distinguishes evidence-supported interpretation from speculation.
- Addresses material limitations and their consequences.
- States implications at the same scope as the design and data.
- Does not introduce new study results.

## Conclusion

- Synthesizes the answer rather than listing results again.
- States the contribution at a warranted level of certainty.
- Introduces no new evidence or analysis.
- Uses citations only if the genre or venue requires them; there is no generic
  citation quota.
- Fits the venue's convention for a separate or integrated conclusion.

## Reviews and syntheses

- Organize literature by a question, concept, method, chronology, or other
  defensible logic rather than by source-by-source summary.
- State search and selection procedures when the review genre requires them.
- Separate evidence synthesis from the authors' interpretation.
- Make the review's contribution and limits explicit.

## Research proposals

- Establish the problem or need, objectives, approach, feasibility, expected
  contribution, resources, and evaluation plan as required by the call.
- Align each objective with a method, output, and assessment criterion.
- Use measurable objectives where the project permits them.
- Follow the funder's current headings, page limits, and required components.
- Treat sample sentence counts and word ranges as examples only, never defaults.

## Cross-section integrity

- Introduction question → Results answer → Discussion interpretation → Conclusion
  synthesis.
- Every method has a result or an explicit reason it does not.
- Every result has a method.
- Abstract claims exist in the body.
- Claim breadth matches population, setting, design, and evidence.
