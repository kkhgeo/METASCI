# Discussion

Use with [the shared principles](../principles.md), including §§6.3–6.4 on available context, preservation, and disclosure. Apply section-level completeness checks only when the relevant section is supplied.

**Basis notes:** Original IDs point to the archived guide linked below. They identify practical guidance and provenance, not newly verified empirical findings. “Editorial” identifies a new diagnostic or qualification in this revision. Diagnostic questions are aids to judgment, not a mandatory sequence.

## Purpose

Explain how the findings answer the research questions, relate to existing knowledge, and support a contribution within stated limits.

## 1. Evidence and interpretation

### DI1. Develop an answer rather than a second results list

Organize around the study’s questions and principal findings. Recall enough of a finding to anchor interpretation, then explain what follows from it.

**Answer check:** What does the paragraph add to the reported observation? Does it address a stated objective, or introduce an unrelated argument?

**Basis:** Original section-structure-7, -12, -30; remined-discussion-2, -14. Diagnostic: editorial.

### DI2. Identify the support for an explanation

Separate the observed finding, the proposed explanation, and the evidence connecting them.

**Explanation check:** Was this explanation directly examined in the study, supported as a possibility by relevant literature, or introduced as an untested hypothesis? These bases can coexist; the wording must not turn plausibility into demonstrated mechanism.

Assess whether alternatives could materially change the interpretation.

**Basis:** Original remined-discussion-3, -9, -11, -12; editorial three-part diagnostic.

### DI3. Compare studies on relevant dimensions

Compare setting, population, scale, design, measurements, analysis, and uncertainty where these affect the interpretation.

**Comparison check:** What agrees or differs, and what evidence supports the proposed reason? An adjacent citation is not a comparison, and a difference between studies is not itself proof of its explanation.

Use relevant `Knowledge/` material according to [the source-use guide](source-use.md). Critique specific methodological limitations fairly rather than substituting promotional claims about the present study.

**Basis:** Original remined-discussion-4 and -5; editorial qualification on explanatory evidence.

## 2. Limits and implications

### DI4. Explain what each limitation restricts

Relate a limitation to a particular estimate, mechanism, causal inference, or proposed generalization.

**Limitation check:** Which conclusion changes because of this limitation, and in what way? Describe direction or magnitude only if supported. A generic label such as “small sample” is insufficient without its consequence.

Include relevant unmeasured pathways and alternatives without creating a list of speculative weaknesses.

**Basis:** Original section-structure-3; remined-discussion-10–13. Diagnostic: editorial.

### DI5. Bridge findings to implications

Develop the theoretical, methodological, practical, or policy implications the evidence supports.

**Application check:** What additional assumptions or conditions connect the finding to the proposed action or wider setting? Identify these rather than treating a local association as a ready recommendation.

Link future research to a specific unresolved question or limitation.

**Basis:** Original section-structure-9, -15; remined-discussion-7, -10. Diagnostic: editorial.

## 3. Paragraph review

### DI6. Calibrate interpretation without enforcing a style

Identify the paragraph’s role: interpretation, comparison, mechanism, limitation, or implication. Assess claim strength against its actual evidence, using shared principle §1.3.

Check both under-interpretation and overreach. Failure to detect an effect does not automatically establish its absence. A paragraph need not include every Discussion function.

**Basis:** Original remined-discussion-3, -6, -17; editorial resolution of the original conflicting strength instructions and paragraph-level scope.
