## SW-PROC-002 — outline-before-drafting

- **Rule:** Define the argumentative skeleton of sections and paragraphs before drafting continuous prose.
- **Rationale:** An outline exposes omissions, duplication, and ordering problems before sentence-level drafting creates avoidable rework.
- **Diagnostic check:** Has each section and paragraph been assigned a function and a one-sentence claim before prose drafting begins?
- **Action:** State each section purpose and paragraph claim, settle their order and evidence placement, and only then draft prose.
- **Operations:** plan, draft
- **Stages:** planning, drafting
- **Scales:** process, manuscript, section
- **Sections:** all
- **Severity / evidence:** high / strong
- **Support counts:** 11 slugs, 7 independent source families
- **Evidence judgment:** Several independent articles and institutional guides support the rule, with a consistent rationale of reducing structural rework.
- **Source-bank rule:** `process-3`
- **Supporting sources:** `pautasso-2013`, `koons-2019`, `hon-uf`, `b1-iufro`, `b5-schulzrinne`, `b6-fess`, `tweets-01a`, `tweets-01c`, `tweets-01d`, `tweets-02`, `tweets-04`
- **Exceptions:**
  - Exploratory freewriting may begin without an outline, but map it before incorporating it into the final structure.
  - Do not require a new document-wide outline for a narrowly scoped paragraph edit.

## SW-PROC-003 — define-one-research-question

- **Rule:** Define one clear research question or core message before drafting and use it as the manuscript's selection criterion.
- **Rationale:** A clear question allows the introduction's gap, the methods, the results, and the discussion's answer to form one reasoning chain.
- **Diagnostic check:** Can the central question be stated in one sentence, and does every major section contribute to answering it?
- **Action:** Fix the question or core message in one sentence; remove, relocate, or reserve for another manuscript any point that does not directly contribute.
- **Operations:** plan, draft, review
- **Stages:** planning, drafting, revision
- **Scales:** process, manuscript
- **Sections:** all
- **Severity / evidence:** high / strong
- **Support counts:** 9 slugs, 7 independent source families
- **Evidence judgment:** Independent articles and institutional guides repeatedly support this prerequisite for a coherent research-argument chain.
- **Source-bank rule:** `process-7`
- **Supporting sources:** `perneger-2004`, `fisher-2013`, `hon-uf`, `b1-iufro`, `b3-lund`, `tweets-01a`, `tweets-01c`, `tweets-02`, `saver-2007`
- **Exceptions:**
  - For multiple research questions, define their hierarchy and mappings under one explicit higher-level question.
  - For exploratory research, define the exploratory purpose and analytical scope instead of forcing a confirmatory hypothesis.
