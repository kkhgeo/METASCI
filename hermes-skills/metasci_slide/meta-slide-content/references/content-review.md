# Content Review

Use this reference when reviewing an existing deck, outline, or draft for content structure.

## Review Order

1. Identify the deck promise and whether it is visible in the current sequence.
2. Check whether the audience, setting, and expected decision are clear.
3. Map the narrative order: context, problem, concept, method, evidence, implication, and next step.
4. Assign each slide a job. Mark slides with unclear or multiple jobs.
5. Check title and lead statement quality.
6. Check whether claims have evidence, citations, figures, examples, or source support.
7. Identify overloaded slides that should split, merge, or move to appendix.
8. Write concrete rewrites for representative slides.

## Findings Format

Report findings first, ordered by severity:

```text
Overall Diagnosis:

Highest-Impact Content Issues:
- Slide:
  Issue:
  Why it matters:
  Recommended fix:

Suggested Reorder:

Representative Rewrite:
Slide ID:
Keyword Title:
Lead Statement:
Body Blocks:
Evidence:

Open Questions:
```

## Common Problems

- Agenda labels used as content titles.
- Titles that state a topic but no lead statement explains the point.
- Evidence figures included without a reading path.
- Too many source details placed on the main slide.
- A method slide that skips inputs, outputs, or validation.
- A conclusion slide that introduces a new claim instead of resolving the deck promise.
