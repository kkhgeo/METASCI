# Environmental Science Journal Profiles

Introduction style characteristics of major environmental science journals.
When journal tier is selected in Stage 0, Stage 2 Generation reflects the relevant journal's conventions.

## Tier 1: Nature/Science/PNAS Level

### Nature / Nature Climate Change / Nature Sustainability
- Introduction length: Short and impactful (500-800 words, 3-4 paragraphs)
- Style: Immediate hook in opening, accessibility for broad readership
- Citation density: Low-medium (2-3 per paragraph)
- Features: Preview of results allowed, "Here we show..." pattern
- Gap: Concise and sharp, 1-2 sentences

### Science
- Introduction length: Very short (400-600 words, 2-3 paragraphs)
- Style: Extremely concise, every sentence essential
- Citation density: Low (1-2 per paragraph)
- Features: Minimal background, straight to the point
- Gap: Compressed into 1 sentence

### PNAS
- Introduction length: Medium (600-1000 words, 3-4 paragraphs)
- Style: Slightly more technical than Nature
- Citation density: Medium (3-4 per paragraph)
- Features: Separate "Significance" section reduces Introduction burden

## Tier 2: Field-Top Journals

### Environmental Science & Technology (ES&T)
- Introduction length: Standard (800-1200 words, 4-5 paragraphs)
- Style: Technical but environmental relevance must be explicit
- Citation density: Medium-high (3-5 per paragraph)
- Features: Separate "Environmental Significance" section, regulatory/policy context important
- Gap: Methodological + environmental gaps in parallel

### Global Change Biology (GCB)
- Introduction length: Standard-extended (1000-1500 words, 4-6 paragraphs)
- Style: Ecology + climate change intersection, theoretical framing emphasized
- Citation density: High (4-6 per paragraph)
- Features: Preference for recent meta-analysis citations, spatial scale specification expected
- Gap: Multi-dimensional (empirical + scale + mechanistic)

### Water Resources Research (WRR)
- Introduction length: Standard-extended (1000-1500 words)
- Style: Quantitative, physics-based mechanism emphasis
- Citation density: High (4-7 per paragraph)
- Features: Modeling studies require model development history

### Journal of Geophysical Research (JGR)
- Introduction length: Extended (1200-2000 words, 5-6 paragraphs)
- Style: Detailed literature review, methodological background included
- Citation density: High (5-8 per paragraph)
- Features: Explicit hypothesis expected, numbered research questions

## Tier 3: Specialized Journals

### General Characteristics
- Introduction length: Extended (1200-2000 words)
- Style: Targeting field specialists, minimal term definitions
- Citation density: High
- Features: Detailed background, systematic literature review character

## Usage

In Stage 2 Generator, adjust based on user-selected journal tier:
- Paragraph count and length
- Citation density
- Background detail level
- Opening style (hook vs. technical narrative)
