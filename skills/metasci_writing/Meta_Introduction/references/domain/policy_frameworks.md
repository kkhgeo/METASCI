# Policy & SDGs Connection Guide for Environmental Science

Connecting to policy and societal implications in an environmental science Introduction is a key means of establishing research urgency and relevance.
Top-tier journals (Nature Climate Change, ES&T, Global Change Biology) expect this.

## UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) Mapping

| SDG | Related Environmental Science Topics |
|-----|--------------------------------------|
| SDG 6 (Clean Water) | Water quality, watershed management, groundwater contamination |
| SDG 7 (Affordable Energy) | Renewable energy, biomass, energy transition |
| SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities) | Urban heat island, air quality, green spaces |
| SDG 13 (Climate Action) | Climate change mitigation/adaptation, carbon cycling |
| SDG 14 (Life Below Water) | Ocean acidification, fisheries, marine pollution |
| SDG 15 (Life on Land) | Biodiversity, forests, soil health |

## Major International Agreements & Frameworks

| Framework | Usage Context |
|-----------|---------------|
| Paris Agreement | Temperature targets, carbon neutrality, NDCs |
| IPCC AR6 | Climate science status, scenarios (SSPs) |
| CBD/Kunming-Montreal Framework | Biodiversity targets, 30x30 |
| UNFCCC | Climate change governance |
| Ramsar Convention | Wetland conservation |
| Stockholm/Basel Conventions | Chemical substances, waste management |

## Placement in Introduction

### P1 (Grand Context) — Policy Urgency Connection
```
"The [phenomenon] poses critical challenges for achieving SDG [N],
with [quantitative impact] threatening [target]..."

"Under the Paris Agreement's goal of limiting warming to 1.5°C,
understanding [process] has become an urgent scientific priority..."
```

### P3 (Gap) — Mismatch between policy needs and scientific knowledge
```
"Despite policy frameworks such as [framework] requiring [action],
the scientific basis for [specific decision] remains insufficient
due to [knowledge gap]..."
```

### P4-P5 (Study Positioning) — Policy contribution
```
"Our findings provide the empirical foundation needed for [policy application],
directly informing [specific management/policy decision]..."
```
