### `section-structure-11` — convergence 9
The conclusion synthesizes points already made and delivers a final judgement; introduce no new information there.

> "Only information included in the main body of the text should be referred to in the conclusion section. Do not introduce new information in the conclusion."

### `section-structure-54` — convergence 1
A one-line conclusion summarizing the main finding suffices in the abstract — optionally one more sentence on implications — and it must relate directly to the main objective and endpoint.

> "A one-line conclusion summarizing your main finding is sufficient, with perhaps a short sentence with the implications for future research, if you have enough space. The conclusion should be directly related to the main objective and endpoint"

**Sources (1):** ecarnot-2015
