### `section-structure-39` — convergence 4
Reserve authorship for those meeting all contribution criteria; agree order and acknowledge non-author contributions explicitly.

> "authorship credit must be reserved for individuals who have met each of the following conditions: 1) made a significant intellectual contribution ... 2) contributed to drafting the article or reviewing and/or revising it for intellectual content, and 3) approved the final version of the manuscript, including references."

**Sources (4):** b5-schulzrinne, fisher-2013, b3-lund, b1-iufro

### `section-structure-44` — convergence 2
Acknowledge non-author contributors and list every funding source in the wording and grant number the funder requires.
