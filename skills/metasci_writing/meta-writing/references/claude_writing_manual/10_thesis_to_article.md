### `section-structure-65` — convergence 1
Never submit a thesis or class paper as is — analyze which content fits the type of article the journal publishes and rework it, narrowing to a single aspect if necessary.

> "Begin by analyzing the content to see what fits with the type of content a particular journal publishes. You may need to focus on only one aspect of a thesis or dissertation."
